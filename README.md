# V Star [Space Settlement Contest 2021 | NSS (National Space Society) Jan 2021]

I won the Third Prize worldwide for my individual project, V Star, when I was in 10th Grade. This is an Individual project based on an idea of a
space colony that is positioned in the Lagrange Point L2 which can work completely without any external support. For this, I was awarded a
Third Prize worldwide alongside two other groups (But the only Third Position in the Individual Category) and was invited to the John F.
Kennedy Space Center to present the project and receive the award. However, due to COVID-19 limitations, the entire ceremony was
cancelled and the prizes were mailed to us. 

This Project included advanced details that were needed to sustain life on the Giant Space Station, my main focus was on the assembly of
the Space Colony in space and how the systems were built after that point. I made a systematic approach on how the Space Colony is
supposed to be built. From there on, my second priority was on the sustainability of Humans and Crops, for which I had explain how oxygen
supply and artificial gravity is generated. My third priority was on Civic System- how people are to be governed, the procedures they need to
follow and the authoritative systems that keep them in check. 

This Project was submitted when I was in 10th grade, however, due to a mistake I was registered in the 12th grade category and yet won the
third position in the Individual Project Category.
